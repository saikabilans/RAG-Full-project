Attendance_policy

Department: HR
Policy Year: 2026

1. Employees are expected to record their attendance through the approved HRMS system.
2. The standard working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM unless otherwise approved.
3. Employees must complete the required sign-in and sign-out records for the working day.
4. Attendance corrections should be reported to HR when an entry is missing or incorrect.
